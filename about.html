About Pul

Personal finance, built around your FIS

Pul Planning is an educational platform that helps you see whether your money is working toward financial independence — starting with one clear number: your Financial Independence Score (FIS).

Who we are

Pul Planning (pul.llc) is a web app for people who earn, spend, and invest in a Nigerian context — Naira by default, PENCOM-aware retirement tools, tax estimates aligned with the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 framework, and everyday categories that match real life (power, school fees, family support, and more).

What we believe

Clarity over complexity

You should know your net worth, cash flow, and FIS without a finance degree.

Independence as a north star

FIS asks a simple question: can passive income cover your outflows? That focus shapes the product.

Education, not advice

We are not a broker, bank, or licensed investment adviser. Tools organise your data so you can decide with eyes open.

What Pul includes

  • Dashboard with FIS, net worth, cash flow, and savings rate
  • Income, expenses, budget (including 50/30/20-style views)
  • Assets, liabilities, debt planner, emergency fund, goals
  • Retirement / RSA planner, insurance needs, estate checklist
  • Calculators (salary/PAYE, Zakat, FI calculator, and more)
  • Reports you can print or share (with clear educational disclaimers)

Privacy & trust

Your financial entries are stored in your account with industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest (via our backend providers). We do not sell your personal financial data. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
