How it works

One score. Your path to independence.

Pul is built around the Financial Independence Score (FIS) — our branded primary KPI that shows how much of your monthly life costs are already covered by passive income. Everything else in the app feeds that clarity.

Why FIS is the centre of Pul

Net worth, budgets, and debt plans matter — but they don’t answer the independence question by themselves. FIS does: If active work income stopped, how much of your lifestyle would passive streams still fund?

< 50%

Building. Passive income covers a small share of outflows. Focus on logging costs and starting passive streams.

50–99%

Halfway. You’re closing the gap. Grow passive income and tighten non-discretionary spend.

100%+

Financially independent on the numbers you’ve entered — passive covers outflows.

What feeds your FIS

  • Passive income — e.g. rent, dividends, interest, pension-style draws you classify as passive
  • Monthly outflows — expenses and lifestyle costs you’ve recorded
  • Not the same as net worth — you can be “asset rich” with a low FIS if little income is passive

Built for a Nigerian context

Currency defaults to NGN (multi-currency display available), retirement tools understand PENCOM / RSA concepts, tax helpers reflect Nigeria Tax Act 2025 style brackets and reliefs, and categories match local life — not a US-only budget template.
