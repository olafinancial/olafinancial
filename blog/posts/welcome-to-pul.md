# Welcome to Pul Planning

Pul Planning is a **personal finance platform built for Nigeria** — and for Nigerians anywhere who still think in Naira, PENCOM pensions, and everyday cash-flow reality.

## What you can do

- Track **income, expenses, assets, and debts** in one place  
- See your **net worth** and cash flow clearly  
- Plan **debt payoff**, **emergency funds**, **retirement (RSA)**, and **goals**  
- Measure progress with the **Financial Independence Score (FIS)**

## Educational, not advice

Pul is an **educational and administrative** tool. It is not a licensed investment adviser, broker, or bank. Numbers and projections are estimates — use them to organise your thinking, then decide with professionals when you need personalised advice.

## Start here

1. [Create a free account](../index.html#/signup)  
2. Complete the short setup wizard  
3. Add income and a few real expenses  
4. Open the **Dashboard** and check your **FIS**
